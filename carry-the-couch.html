How to carry

WASD / arrowswalk E or Spacegrab the nearest thing / let go hold Shiftlift what you're holding 1234shout LIFT! DROP! PIVOT! GO! to your crew
Same keyboard: mover 1 is WASD E Left Shift, mover 2 is arrows Enter Right Shift. Phones: left thumb walks, GRAB and LIFT on the right.
🏋️Heavy things need two lifters. A couch only comes off the floor when everyone it needs is holding Shift. One mover can't even drag it.
🪵Dragging is slow and scratches wood floors (that comes out of your deposit). Lifting is fast but tires your arms. Put it down to get your grip back.
🪜Stairs: lift or it slides. Anything left on the floor on a staircase goes down on its own and smashes. The mover at the bottom of the stairs tires fastest, so rest on the landings.
🔄Pivot. A long couch won't turn in a hallway. Let one end stand still while the other swings, back up, try another angle.
💥Bumps cost money. Hitting walls dents the furniture and the building. Glass, lamps and TVs crack fast.
🚚Load it. It counts when the whole thing is inside the van.
