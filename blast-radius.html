HOW TO PLAY

You each get a tank on opposite sides of a destructible battlefield. Take turns lobbing shells. Land one close enough and the blast takes a chunk out of them — and out of the ground they're standing on. First to win 2 rounds takes the match.
On a phone or tablet: drag anywhere on the battlefield to aim — direction aims, distance from your tank is power. ◀ ▶ drive, ▲ ▼ nudge the angle one degree, and hold FIRE to charge, release to shoot.

Finding the range. The green tick on the power bar is the power that would reach them at your current angle in still air over flat ground — a starting table, not a solution. Correcting for the wind and for the hill in the way is the game. The white tick marks the power you last fired at, and each shot is called short or long so you know which way to move.
SHELL — unlimited, reliable.
CLUSTER — bursts into five bomblets. Wide, shallow damage.
DIGGER — burrows into the ground before it blows. Good against dug-in tanks.
ROLLER — runs downhill on impact. Lob it above them and let gravity aim.
NUKE — ends arguments.
The three specials refill at the start of every round. The nuke is one per match.
Wind changes every turn and pushes the shell the whole way — watch the gauge and the pennant on your tank. Tanks take falling damage when the ground goes out from under them. In two-player games a 45-second clock runs on your turn and fires whatever the HUD is showing when it expires; solo play is untimed.
