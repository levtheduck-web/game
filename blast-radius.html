HOW TO PLAY

You each get a tank on opposite sides of a destructible battlefield. Take turns lobbing shells. Land one close enough and the blast takes a chunk out of them — and out of the ground they're standing on. First to win 2 rounds takes the match.
← →Drive (costs fuel) ↑ ↓Aim the barrel Hold SPACECharge power — release to fire 1 – 5Pick a weapon MouseDrag from your tank to aim and fire TabShow last shot's arc
SHELL — unlimited, reliable.
CLUSTER — bursts into five bomblets. Wide, shallow damage.
DIGGER — burrows into the ground before it blows. Good against dug-in tanks.
ROLLER — rolls downhill on impact. Lob it above them and let gravity aim.
NUKE — one per match. Ends arguments.
Watch the wind gauge — it changes every turn and pushes your shot the whole way. Tanks take falling damage when the ground goes out from under them.
