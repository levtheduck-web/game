PATCH NOTES
A card battler where you never buy cards.
You buy balance changes — and they apply to both decks.
1. Fight an opponent. Both sides play from a 10-card deck under identical rules.
2. Win, and the game patches. You take one balance change. Your next opponent takes one too.
3. Patches are global and permanent. Nerfing FIRE nerfs your FIRE.
4. Then you rebuild — swap up to 2 cards to exploit the rules you just wrote.
5. Survive 9 opponents and the build ships as v1.0.
