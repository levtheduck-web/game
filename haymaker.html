HOW TO PLAY

W A S DMove, relative to the screen — you always face your opponent. On the broadcast camera D steps in, A backs off, W / S circle. JJab — fast and cheap. Mouse left also jabs. KCross — straight right hand. Mouse right also crosses. LHook — short range, big damage, hard to block. IUppercut — very short range. Breaks a guard and punishes ducking. HOLD HTHE HAYMAKER. Hold to wind up, let go to throw. A full charge breaks any guard and sends them flying — but they can see it coming. Dodge cancels the wind-up. SPACEHold to block. Stops most of a straight punch, less of a hook, and costs stamina. Tap it just as a punch lands for a PERFECT block: no damage, they stagger, your counter is ready. SHIFTDodge in the direction you're moving — on its own you duck. Dodge a punch and your next one is a COUNTER (×1.6). Q · EYour fighter's two abilities (they recharge). RUltimate. Fill the purple meter by landing, blocking and dodging. TTaunt. Finish it untouched for a chunk of meter — get hit and it hurts more. V · MSwitch camera (broadcast or behind you) · mute everything. ESCPause

THE FIGHT

Three rounds of a minute. Drop to zero health and you're down — mash punch to beat the ten-count. Three knockdowns is a TKO. Still standing at the end? The judges score each round on damage and knockdowns.

Punches cost stamina. Throw too many and you gas out: slower, weaker, and your guard crumbles. Chain punches together for a combo bonus. Under a quarter health you go into RAGE: punches hit 15% harder.
