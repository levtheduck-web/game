How to play

One rule: if the sea touches the shell ring around your tower, the flag falls and the game is over. Each tide comes in a little higher than the last, and the last wave of every tide is a big one.

1 SpadeDig up sand, up to six scoops. The hole you leave is a moat, and moats catch water.
2 PileDrop a scoop. Walls are made of piles. Sand dug near the sea comes up wet.
3 PatPack the sand down. Packed sand stands steep and stands up to waves. Dry sand won't pack.
4 BucketFill it in the sea, then pour it on dry sand. Damp sand holds a slope; dry sand slides.

Shell shop: shells wash up after every tide, and holding a tide pays everyone 2. Press B to buy a bigger pail, a faster spade, a flat paddle, a bigger bucket, wellies, or driftwood logs (5 lays one across where you face; the sea can't move it).

What works: a ring wall, wet and patted, with a moat on the sea side. A loose dry heap washes off in two waves.

Controls: move with WASD or arrows, aim with the mouse, hold the mouse button or Space to use your tool. Switch tools with 1-4, Q/E or the wheel. On a phone: stick on the left, USE on the right, and you aim where you face.
