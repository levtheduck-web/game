HOW TO PLAY

  • Your cycle moves forward on its own. You only ever steer left or right.
  • Every cycle paints a solid wall behind it. Touch any wall — yours, theirs, the arena edge — and your round is over.
  • Outlive someone and you score. The last rider alive takes the round bonus.
  • The arena closes in after twenty seconds, so camping in a corner won't save you.
