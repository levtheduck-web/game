How to play

Your stall has three menu slots. Tap a slot's dish to change it. Each dish needs two ingredients from the wholesaler. Press Cook (or 1 2 3) and a batch is ready a few seconds later on one of your two burners.

Customers each want one dish most tonight, and the hourly craze is the most wanted. They pick a stall by that dish, your price, how long your line is, whether something is sizzling, and your stars. Ring the bell (B) to pull in walkers nearby.

Undercut a rival and their line walks to you. But you earn less, and a long line sends people next door. Selling the dish nobody else sells means nobody can undercut you.

Food goes stale. Fresh food earns stars, stale food loses them, and old food gets thrown out. Cook to the line, not to the burner. People who wait too long walk off and cost you a star.

The Family (F): hire goons and send them to a rival. 🪳 Cockroaches scare customers off until the owner stomps them. ☠️ Poison turns their counter food bad, so people get a tummy ache. 🕵️ A tip-off sends the health inspector: a stall with roaches or bad food gets boarded up, and three strikes shuts it down for the night. A 📹 camera catches goons, and a 🐈 cat eats roaches.

Under attack? Stomp roaches (Space) and bin bad food (X) before the inspector arrives.

Build up (U): cart, stall, shop, restaurant, mega tower. More burners, a fourth menu slot, faster service, more customers.

The wholesaler is shared. Every pack anyone buys pushes that price up for the whole street for a while. Stocking up early beats paying the rush price.
