How to play

You are the chef. On a computer: click to look around with the mouse, WASD to walk, Shift to run, Space to jump, E or click to use what you look at, Tab to free the mouse. On a phone: your left thumb walks, drag on the right to look, tap things (or the big button) to use them.

Your computer 🖥️ sits on the desk beside your back door. Walk up to it and press E (or tap it) to change your menu and prices, buy ingredients, plan Family jobs, build up and check on the other stalls.

Cooking: look at a free wok and press E to cook what your line wants most, or 1 2 3 for a dish. When the ❗ pops up, toss it (E): it cooks faster and earns better tips. When it's done, plate it before it burns. Food on the counter sells by itself. Cook buys any missing ingredients itself.

Easy gives you a kitchen helper who keeps cooking while you are out of the kitchen and saves food you forget, and the CPU stalls leave you alone.

Customers each want one dish most tonight, and the hourly craze is the most wanted. They pick a stall by that dish, your price, how long your line is, whether something is sizzling, and your stars. Ring the bell on your counter (look at it and press E, or tap it) to pull in walkers nearby. Change prices and dishes on your computer.

Undercut a rival and their line walks to you. But you earn less, and a long line sends people next door. Fresh food earns stars, stale food loses them, and old food gets thrown out.

Sneaking out: your back door is behind you, on your left. It opens into your back yard; the gate at the back leads to the lane behind every stall, and alleys between the stalls lead to the street, where you'll see the other chefs. Every rival's yard has spots that glow when you get close: 🪳 tip their bins over, 💨 a stink bomb down the vent, 🔌 their gas, ☠️ spoil their ice chest, 🌶️ ghost pepper sauce, 👛 their cash tin, 🕵️ the payphone to call the health inspector. Hold E there until it's done. While you're out, nobody cooks at your stall (unless your helper does).

Getting caught: if a stall's owner catches you in their back yard (E on you), you're chased home and have to lie low for a bit. A 🐕 guard dog and a 📹 camera can catch you too. When someone sneaks into your yard, the map flashes red: go out the back and catch them. A 🐈 cat eats roaches. Stomp roaches in your kitchen (E) and bin bad food (X) before the inspector comes: three strikes shuts a stall down for the night.

Build up (on your computer): cart, stall, shop, restaurant, mega tower. More woks, a fourth menu slot, faster service, more customers. Bigger stalls unlock the Boss's favor (Family, on your computer).

The wholesaler is shared. Every pack anyone buys pushes that price up for the whole street for a while. Stocking up early beats paying the rush price.
