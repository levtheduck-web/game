TERMS OF SERVICE

1. Be decent

  • No bullying, hate, threats or harassing other players.
  • No cheating, exploiting bugs on purpose, or spamming invites and reports.
  • Pick a nametag and icon that are OK for everyone - and don't use your real name.

2. What the owner can see, and why

To keep GLITCHBOX safe, the owner can see some things about you on GLITCHBOX from the owner console:

  • whether you're online and which game you're playing right now. GLITCHBOX doesn't keep a record of which games you played or for how long.
  • your nametag, the name on your Google account, your email address and friend code. GLITCHBOX doesn't keep your Google photo.
  • your friends, reports made by or about you, tokens you send or get, and cloud saves
  • the kind of device you use (like "Chromebook" or "iPhone"), and a scrambled code for your internet connection. Your IP address itself is never stored, so nobody can read it, not even the owner.

All of this is used only for moderation: keeping players safe and stopping cheating, bullying and spam. It is never used for anything else, never sold and never shared, and nobody looks through it just to watch what you do. It only covers what you do on GLITCHBOX, nothing else on your device or anywhere else online.

3. What other players can see

  • Your friends see whether you're online, which game you're playing right now (so they can join you), and your profile: icon, badges and best scores. You can hide what you're playing from friends in your profile.
  • Everyone else only ever sees your nametag and your arcade icon - never your real name or your email.
  • Leaderboards show your nametag, your icon and your best scores.

4. Moderation

The owner can message you, kick you back to the hub, switch games off, or ban your account, your device or your internet connection, for a while or for good, if you break these rules.

The owner can pick moderators - trusted players who can see your nametag, icon, friend code, which game you're playing right now, reports about you and past bans. Moderators never see your real name, email, internet connection or device, and they use what they see only for moderation too.

Moderators can message you and kick you back to the hub. They can't ban anyone on their own: a moderator can only ask the owner to ban someone, and the ban only happens if the owner approves it.

If you're banned you can send an appeal. A moderator can turn an appeal down, but only the owner can lift a ban.

5. Your account and your data

You sign in with Google. GLITCHBOX keeps your Google name and email privately, so the owner can tell accounts apart. Only the owner can see them; other players and moderators only see the nametag you choose. My Data in the menu shows everything GLITCHBOX keeps about you, lets you download it, and lets you delete your account - that erases everything about you. Guests can see and erase their data there too.

6. Tokens and unlocks

Tokens are free: you earn them by playing, and they never cost real money. You can send tokens to your friends (up to 500 at a time and 1000 a day), but you can't sell them or swap them for anything outside GLITCHBOX.

Tokens, unlocked games and icons aren't worth real money. The owner won't reset them for no reason, and if a reset is ever needed it will be announced first. Tokens gotten by cheating can be taken away.

7. Changes

If these terms change, you'll be asked to agree again before you keep playing.

If you're under 13, ask a parent or guardian to read this with you first.
